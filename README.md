# MKR Global | Web3 Donations

MKR Global is a borderless Web3 initiative built to support natural elements and fundamental human rights with 100% cryptographic transparency. 

## The Problem & Our Solution
Humanity and nature are suffering immensely from global crises. Traditional philanthropic systems are often opaque, slow, and burdened by heavy administrative fees that prevent funds from reaching their intended destinations. 

MKR Global bridges Web3 technology with real-world impact. By utilizing decentralized networks, we route contributions directly to publicly auditable destinations. 
* **0% Platform Fees:** We take zero cut from the donations.
* **Cryptographic Transparency:** Every inbound donation and outbound distribution is verifiable on-chain.
* **Multichain Infrastructure:** We operate across Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Polygon, Base, and Monad to ensure frictionless, low-cost participation.

## Technical Architecture
The MKR Global decentralized application is a lightweight, highly accessible frontend designed to interact seamlessly with Web3 injected providers.
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
* **Wallet Integration:** Support for MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, and HaHa Wallet.
* **Treasury Routing:** All donations are routed to the MKR Global Master Vault (`0x7811334586e85540f1DAE69780dEA0Db7bb45838`).

## Fund Distribution Protocol
We currently support 11 Verified Causes categorized into two primary sectors:
1. **🌱 Restoring Nature:** Preservation and ecological restoration initiatives.
2. **🤝 Empowering Humanity:** Direct, borderless aid for fundamental human needs.

Donation and distribution histories are reconciled and published weekly. Funds held in the Master Vault are batched and transferred directly to the verified on-chain addresses of the partnered causes, establishing a perfect, immutable audit trail.
